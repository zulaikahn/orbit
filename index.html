What does the beta include?

The Windows app includes Zunavo’s own playback engine and video conversion tools. Stremio does not need to be installed. This is an unsigned development build; playback reliability is still being tested.

Does every title have a playable video?

No. A title in the catalogue is a discovery result, not a promise of playback. Zunavo does not include a licensed film or TV subscription. Connected services may return no video or fail to connect.

Where are my watchlist and progress saved?

They stay locally in this app on your computer. Account sync is not included in this beta.

Can I watch in my browser or on my phone?

Open the mobile web preview in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android. You can add it to your home screen. It includes search, a watchlist and compatible direct-video playback. Native app-store apps and mobile torrent playback are not included yet.
